## Item 1A. Risk Factors

Demand for semiconductor components depends on capital spending by electronics and industrial customers. A reduction in customer orders could reduce utilization and pressure gross margins.

We rely on specialized foundry and packaging partners for certain advanced components. Capacity constraints or quality issues could delay shipments and increase costs.

Our international operations expose results to foreign exchange movements and changing trade requirements.
