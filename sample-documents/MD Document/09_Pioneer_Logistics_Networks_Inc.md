## Item 1A. Risk Factors

Freight volumes depend on industrial production, retail demand, and inventory levels. A downturn in shipment volumes could reduce revenue.

Fuel prices, labor costs, and driver availability can materially affect transportation expenses and operating margins.

Our logistics network relies on third-party carriers, warehouse operators, and technology systems. Disruptions could affect service levels.
