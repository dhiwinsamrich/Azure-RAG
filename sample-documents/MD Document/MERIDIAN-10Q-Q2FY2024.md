## Item 2. Management's Discussion and Analysis

Total revenue was $684 million for the three months ended June 30, 2024, an
increase of 9% compared with $627 million for the three months ended June 30,
2023. The increase was driven by higher patient volumes at our outpatient
surgical centers and a rate increase negotiated with our largest commercial
payer effective January 1, 2024.

Gross margin was 38.1% for Q2 fiscal year 2024, compared with 36.9% for Q2
fiscal year 2023, an improvement of approximately 120 basis points, reflecting
lower agency staffing costs as permanent nurse hiring improved.

Operating income was $71 million for the quarter, compared with $58 million
in the prior-year quarter. Operating expenses grew 7% year over year, driven
by continued investment in our electronic health records platform.

Net income for the quarter was $49 million, or $0.62 per diluted share,
compared with net income of $39 million, or $0.50 per diluted share, in the
three months ended June 30, 2023.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our revenue depends significantly on reimbursement rates set by government
payers and negotiated with commercial insurers. A reduction in reimbursement
rates, or a shift in patient mix toward lower-reimbursement payers, could
reduce revenue and operating margin.

We face a competitive labor market for registered nurses and other clinical
staff. Continued wage inflation or a return to elevated agency staffing usage
could increase operating expenses faster than revenue.

We are subject to extensive federal and state healthcare regulation. Changes
in reimbursement policy or new regulatory requirements could increase
compliance costs or affect the way we deliver and bill for care.
