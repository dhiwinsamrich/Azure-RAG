## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis

Total revenue was $1,842 million in fiscal year 2024, an increase of 18%
compared with $1,561 million in fiscal year 2023. Growth was driven primarily
by the Industrial Automation segment, which benefited from strong demand for
warehouse picking robots, partially offset by a slower ramp in the Consumer
Robotics segment.

Gross margin was 54.2% in fiscal year 2024, compared with 51.6% in fiscal year
2023, an improvement of approximately 260 basis points. The improvement
reflects manufacturing scale efficiencies at our Reno facility and a
favourable shift in revenue mix toward higher-margin service contracts.

Operating income was $312 million in fiscal year 2024, compared with $224
million in fiscal year 2023. Operating expenses grew 14% year over year,
reflecting continued investment in research and development for our
next-generation actuator platform.

Net income for fiscal year 2024 was $241 million, or $1.87 per diluted share,
compared with net income of $173 million, or $1.36 per diluted share, in
fiscal year 2023.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business depends on continued capital spending by logistics and
manufacturing customers on automation equipment. A slowdown in industrial
capital expenditure could reduce revenue growth and compress margins. We face
intense competition from both established industrial automation vendors and
well-funded robotics startups.

We rely on a small number of suppliers for precision actuators and motion
controllers used in our robots. A disruption at any of these suppliers could
delay shipments and harm our results of operations.

Currency fluctuations may affect reported results, as a growing portion of
revenue is denominated in currencies other than the US dollar as we expand in
Europe and Asia.
