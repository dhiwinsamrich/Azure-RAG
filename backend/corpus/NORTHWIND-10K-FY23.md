## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis

Total revenue was $211,915 million in fiscal year 2023, an increase of 7%
compared with $198,270 million in fiscal year 2022. Growth was driven primarily
by the Intelligent Cloud segment, partially offset by softness in the More
Personal Computing segment.

Gross margin was 69.8% in fiscal year 2023, compared with 68.4% in fiscal year
2022, an improvement of approximately 140 basis points. The improvement
reflects a favourable shift in revenue mix toward higher-margin cloud services,
partially offset by increased datacenter depreciation.

Operating expenses grew 9% year over year, reflecting continued investment in
research and development and in cloud infrastructure capacity.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business depends on continued demand for cloud services. A slowdown in
enterprise technology spending could reduce revenue growth and compress
margins. We face intense competition in every market in which we operate.

Currency fluctuations may affect reported results, as a significant portion of
revenue is denominated in currencies other than the US dollar.
